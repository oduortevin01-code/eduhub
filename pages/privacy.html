🔒 Privacy Policy

What we collect

Your name, email, and study level, so Zimora can tailor notes and mock papers to your syllabus.

How your password is protected

Passwords are hashed before storage — Zimora never sees or stores your password in readable form.

Uploads

Photos, files, and videos you upload are processed to help answer your questions and are only visible to your account.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data to third parties.

Your control

You can request deletion of your account and data at any time from Settings.
